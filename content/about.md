## About me

Hey there! I’m a Munich-based Senior Cloud Engineer and Architect with a passion for building resilient, high-availability platforms through code. Armed with four Google Cloud certifications and Kubernetes credentials (CKA and CKAD), I spend my days architecting GCP landing zones, tuning GKE clusters with Terraform, and orchestrating 24/7 SLA cloud infrastructure.  In the rare event of a global network apocalypse—or when I'm taking a break from incident management and FinOps reviews—you’ll catch me shredding up fresh powder in the Alps, kitesurfing across my local lake, or whipping up a Parmesan-soaked eggplant lasagna for my friends to enjoy as we ride out the digital storm.  

Welcome to my blog (notes.linderttobias.com), where I share technical cloud insights, project notes, and personal updates from my everyday life.
